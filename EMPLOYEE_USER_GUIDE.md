# Employee Leave Planner - User Guide

## 📖 Welcome!

This guide will help you use the Employee Leave Planner to manage your 2026 leave requests.

---

## 🔐 Getting Started

### Accessing the Application

1. **Open the leave planner URL** (provided by HR)
2. You'll see a login screen

### Logging In

![Login Screen]

1. **Employee ID**: Enter your employee ID (e.g., `BAYN00002`)
2. **Date of Birth**: Enter in DDMMYYYY format (e.g., `15031990` for March 15, 1990)
3. Click **"Login"**

> ⚠️ **Important**: If you can't login, contact HR for your credentials.

---

## ⚙️ First-Time Setup

### Configuring Manager Email

After your first login:

1. Click **"Settings"** in the top right corner
2. Enter your **manager's email address**
3. Click **"Save"**

This enables automatic email notifications to your manager when you submit leave requests.

---

## 📅 Requesting Leave

### How to Submit a Leave Request

1. Click the **"Request Leave"** button (green button with + icon)
2. **Select dates** on the calendar:
   - Click start date
   - Click end date
   - Selected range will be highlighted in blue
3. **Choose leave type**:
   - Annual Leave (default)
   - Sick Leave
   - Emergency Leave
4. **Optional**: Add notes explaining your request
5. **Optional**: Use offset days (carried over from 2025) if available
6. Click **"Submit Request"**

### Understanding Your Leave Balance

Your leave balance card shows:
- **Total Days Available**: Annual entitlement + carried over days
- **Used Days**: Days you've already taken or requested
- **Remaining Days**: What you have left to use
- **Offset Days**: Special days carried from previous year (if applicable)

---

## 📊 Tracking Your Requests

### Request Status

All your leave requests are shown below the calendar with these statuses:

- 🕒 **Pending**: Waiting for manager approval
- ✅ **Approved**: Manager has approved your request
- ❌ **Rejected**: Request was declined

### Filtering Requests

Use the dropdown filter to view:
- All Requests
- Pending only
- Approved only
- Rejected only

---

## 📱 Using the Calendar

### Understanding Calendar Colors

- **Blue highlighted dates**: Your approved leave
- **Coral/Orange dots**: UAE Public Holidays
- **Gray dates**: Weekends
- **Disabled dates**: Past dates or dates outside 2026

### UAE Public Holidays 2026

The calendar automatically shows all UAE public holidays. These don't count against your leave balance!

Upcoming holidays are listed below the calendar for easy planning.

## 🎯 2025 Leave Balance Confirmation

### What is This?

At the bottom of the page, you'll see your 2025 leave summary showing:
- Leaves taken each month in 2025
- Total leaves for the year
- Your calculated balance

### What You Need to Do

1. **Review the data** carefully
2. If correct: Click **"Confirm Balance"**
3. If incorrect: Click **"Request Changes"** and explain the discrepancy

> 💡 **Important**: If you don't confirm or request changes, the balance will be considered final.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I edit or cancel a submitted request?

Currently, you need to contact your manager directly to modify or cancel a request. A future update will add this feature.

### Why can't I select certain dates?

Dates may be disabled if:
- They're in the past
- They're outside of 2026
- They overlap with another request

### What are "offset days"?

Offset days are leave days carried over from 2025. They're shown separately and can be used instead of your 2026 annual leave.

### Do weekends and public holidays count against my leave?

No! Only business days (Monday-Friday, excluding public holidays) are counted.

### I see a low balance warning. What should I do?

If your balance is less than 5 days:
- Plan remaining leave carefully
- Contact HR if you believe there's an error
- Review your 2025 balance confirmation

### How do I know if my manager received my request?

After submitting, you'll see:
- A success message
- An email notification indicator on your request
- "Manager Notified" badge

## 🚨 Common Issues & Solutions

### Issue: Can't Login

**Solution**: 
1. Check Employee ID format (e.g., BAYN00002)
2. Check Date of Birth format (DDMMYYYY)
3. Contact HR if credentials don't work

### Issue: "Insufficient Balance" Error

**Solution**:
1. Check your remaining balance
2. Consider using offset days if available
3. Reduce the number of days requested
4. Contact HR if balance seems wrong

### Issue: "Overlapping Dates" Error

**Solution**:
1. Check your existing requests
2. Choose different dates
3. Cancel or modify the conflicting request first

### Issue: Manager Email Notification Not Working

**Solution**:
1. Verify manager email is correct in Settings
2. Check for typos in email address
3. Notifications are simulated for now - your manager should still review the system
